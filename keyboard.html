Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2024

Thank you for using the Encrypting Custom Keyboard application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data, keystrokes, or sensitive information such as passwords, credit card details, or messages typed on the keyboard.

However, we use Yandex Ads (an advertising network) to display ads within the app. When ads are shown, Yandex may automatically collect certain data to provide relevant advertising and measure ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device ID – unique identifier for your device (e.g., Android Advertising ID, IDFA).
  • IP Address – to approximate your general location (country / region).
  • Device information – model, OS version, screen size, language settings.
  • App usage data – ad impressions, clicks, and interactions with ads.
  • Non‑personal browsing data – such as referral URLs or browser type (if applicable).
⚡ Important: Yandex Ads processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, store, or have access to any personal data collected by Yandex. The data is used solely for ad targeting, frequency capping, and analytics.

2. Encryption & Security

Our app supports AES‑256 encryption to secure any messages that you choose to encrypt. All encrypted messages are only available to you (the user) and are not stored or transmitted by our application. We implement industry‑standard security measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Yandex Ads to serve advertisements. As described above, Yandex may collect device and usage data for ad personalization and analytics. Yandex is a trusted ad provider and processes data in accordance with its Privacy Policy.

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal information with any other third parties. We do not use additional analytics tools, tracking pixels, or data brokers.

4. Permissions

The Encrypting Custom Keyboard requires certain permissions to function properly:

  • Network State – used to check internet connectivity for loading ads from Yandex.
  • Internet Access – required to display Yandex ads and to serve ad content.

We do not request or use permissions for contacts, location (GPS), camera, microphone, or storage beyond what is necessary for the keyboard functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
