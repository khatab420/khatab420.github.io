Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Veil Browser (the "App") is built and provided by Asmatullah Khataab (referred to as "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information that directly identifies you (such as your name, email address, or phone number) through the App itself.

However, our App uses third-party advertising services (Yandex Mobile Ads) that may automatically collect certain data to serve relevant ads and improve ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers — such as Android Advertising ID (AAID) or other device IDs.
  • IP address — used to determine general location (country/city level) and for fraud prevention.
  • Device information — including device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • App usage data — such as ad impressions, clicks, and interactions with ads.
  • Network information — carrier and connection type (Wi‑Fi / cellular).

⚡ Important: This data is collected and processed by Yandex Mobile Ads (a third-party ad network) in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store this data ourselves — it is used solely by the ad SDK to deliver and improve advertisements.

2. How we use information

The data collected via the advertising SDK is used to:

  • Show you relevant advertisements that match your interests.
  • Measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns.
  • Prevent fraudulent ad activity and ensure the security of the ad ecosystem.
  • Improve the overall ad experience within the App.

We do not use this data for any other purposes, and we do not combine it with any other data that could identify you.

3. Third-party services (Advertising)

Our App integrates the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (application ID: 19649432) to display advertisements. Yandex is a third-party service that operates independently and has its own privacy policy.

We encourage you to review Yandex's privacy practices:

  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads Terms

The App does not use any other third-party analytics, crash reporting, or data collection tools.

4. Data storage & retention

Any data collected by the Yandex Ads SDK is stored and managed by Yandex in accordance with their data retention policies. We do not store any of this data on our servers or have direct access to it.

If you wish to understand how Yandex handles data retention, please refer to their official privacy documentation linked above.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

6. Your choices & rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalised ads — on Android, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based advertising via Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Limit ad tracking — enable the "Opt out of Ads Personalization" option in your device settings.
  • Request information — you may contact us to ask about data practices, though please note that we do not hold any personal data about you.

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions about how your data is handled, please reach out using the contact details below.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
